# __App__ -- spec

One living spec, amended with dated entries. Concepts, clocks, screen x state and
route x actor are pointers to their registries (`concepts.toml`,
`src/__app__/clock.py`, `contracts/registry.py`, `auth_matrix.toml`), never
copies of them.

## Scenarios

Acceptance lines, each with a `step:` id an instrument can pass or fail.

| step | scenario | instrument |
|---|---|---|
| a1 | A member exports the month's entries as CSV from the summary screen. | walk:planted |
| a2 | A member sees their household's month total on the summary screen. | walk:planted |

## Seams

| value | who stamps | who trusts | invariant | test or gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `app.tenant_id` | `Database.tenant()` | every RLS policy | unset or '' reads nothing | `tests/test_rls_unbound.py` |
| `X-Member` | the browser (dev only) | `DevTokenAuth` | absent -> 401, other household -> 403 | `tests/test_auth_matrix.py` |
| `AS_OF` | `settings.toml` | server, walk runner | one row, read by both | `tests/test_settings_parity.py` |

## Failure modes

| what fails | user-visible | detected by |
|---|---|---|
| database down | `disabled_503` state | `frontend/src/screens/summary/states.test.tsx` |
| a request hangs | `failed` state after REQUEST_TIMEOUT_MS | `states.test.tsx` |
